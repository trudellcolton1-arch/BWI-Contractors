Built different, on purpose.

The name is the promise: BWI stands for Building with Integrity — a minority woman-owned general contractor headquartered in Arlington, Texas, steps from the ballpark, at the center of the metroplex we build for.

For more than twenty years we've delivered projects the way owners wish every contractor would: with seamless communication, disciplined on-site organization, and workmanship that holds up long after the ribbon is cut. Fully licensed, bonded and insured, we manage risk so you don't have to think about it.

Accountability, in writing

Licensed, bonded and insured on every engagement. Scope, schedule and budget live in documents — not in someone's head.

Communication that ships

Craft you can inspect
